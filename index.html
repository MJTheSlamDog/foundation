WHO WE ARE

People first. Community always.

Nomalinge Nokenke Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organisation working to uplift communities in South Africa, particularly in the Eastern Cape.

Our work brings together youth development, educational support, sport and recreation, community outreach, skills and entrepreneurship, poverty alleviation, health and wellness, and strategic partnerships.
