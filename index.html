WHO WE ARE

People first. Community always.

Nomalinge Nokenke Foundation is a community-driven non-profit organisation working to uplift communities in South Africa, particularly in the Eastern Cape.

Our work brings together youth development, educational support, sport and recreation, community outreach, skills and entrepreneurship, poverty alleviation, health and wellness, and strategic partnerships.

03

Sports & Recreation

Active bodies. Stronger communities. Brighter futures.

We use sport and recreation as a powerful tool for upliftment across South Africa. We make safe, inclusive activities available to everyone — rural, urban, township and remote areas — because health, confidence and opportunity should never be limited by where you live.

What we do:

  • Community Leagues & Tournaments – Soccer, netball, basketball, volleyball, athletics, cricket and more — for all ages, genders and abilities.
  • Youth Talent Development – Coaching, clinics, academies, and clear pathways to clubs, federations, bursaries and competitive opportunities.
  • Wellness & Active Living – Fitness programmes, outdoor recreation, walking clubs, health awareness and lifestyle education.
  • Inclusive & Adaptive Sport – Programmes for people with disabilities, seniors, women, and groups often left out.
  • Infrastructure & Resources – Equipping fields, courts and safe spaces; setting up equipment libraries so no-one is excluded for lack of gear.

Our Goal: By 2030, sport and recreation will be a core pillar of our work — building healthy, united, confident communities in every province.

04

05

06
